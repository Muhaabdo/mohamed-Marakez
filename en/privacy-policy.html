Your privacy matters to us. This page explains how we collect, use and protect your data when you contact us or leave your details through globalmarketingrealestate.com, operated by Global Marketing Real Estate — the independent real estate marketing and media platform owned and operated by Mohamed El Sabagh. By using the website, you agree to the practices described below.

1 Data we collect

When you leave your details or contact us, we may collect:

  • Contact details: your name, mobile / WhatsApp number, and email if you provide it.
  • Interest details: the project or unit type you ask about and your approximate budget.
  • Technical data: device and browser type and the pages you visit, collected automatically to improve the service.

2 How we use your data

We use the data only for these purposes:

  • Contacting you to answer your enquiry about real estate projects.
  • Recommending units or projects that match your interest and budget.
  • Sending property-related updates or offers (you can stop these at any time).
  • Improving the website experience and service quality.

3 Data sharing

We do not sell your data to anyone. As an independent real estate marketing platform, we may share your data only with the developer of the project you are interested in, in order to complete the reservation process or answer your enquiry, and only for the purpose you contacted us about.

4 Cookies

The website uses basic cookies to run the page correctly and measure advertising campaign performance. You can control cookies from your browser settings; note that disabling them may affect some website functions.

5 Protecting your data

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access or use. Although we aim for the highest level of protection, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we make every effort to keep your data confidential.

6 Your rights

At any time you may:

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Request that your data be amended or corrected.
  • Request that your data be deleted from our records.
  • Stop receiving marketing messages.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us on the WhatsApp number below.

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any change will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date at the top, so please review it periodically.
