About the project RAMLA

Ramla — the exclusive coastal destination on the Ras El Hekma beach

Ramla is the integrated coastal destination by Marakez in Ras El Hekma, with a white sandy beach stretching 1.4 kilometers, designed to balance privacy with a lively community. The project has two residential neighborhoods: The Town, with walkways linking private villas, and The Grove, with wide green spaces and larger villas.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Ramla Ras El Hekma

Where exactly is Ramla?

Ramla is on the Ras El Hekma beach on Egypt's North Coast: 10 minutes from Caesar, 20 minutes from Almaza Bay and 35 minutes from Hacienda Red.

Who is the developer of Ramla?

The developer is Marakez, which also developed District 5 and Crescent Walk in New Cairo and Shams Soma in Soma Bay.

What unit types and sizes are available at Ramla?

Ramla offers chalets from 100 to 207 m², townhouses from 173 to 201 m², twin houses from 152 to 273 m² and standalone villas from 155 to 313 m², all fully finished.

What do Ramla prices start from?

Ramla chalets start from EGP 22,176,000, townhouses from EGP 39,273,000, twin houses from EGP 40,864,000 and standalone villas from EGP 47,311,000. Prices are indicative and may be changed by the developer.

What are the payment plans at Ramla?

Down payment from 5% with installments up to 8 years.

What facilities and services does Ramla have?

A 1.4 km sandy beach, a lagoon, a hotel, a sports complex, a community farm and Village Street.

What is the difference between Ramla and Shams Soma?

Both are developed by Marakez. Ramla is in Ras El Hekma on the North Coast, while Shams Soma is in Soma Bay on the Red Sea. See Shams Soma details and prices.
