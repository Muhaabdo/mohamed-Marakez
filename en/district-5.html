FAQ

Frequently asked questions about District 5

Are there ready-to-move units at District 5?

Yes. District 5 has ready-to-move apartments with a 10% down payment and installments over 7 years.

Where exactly is District 5?

District 5 is in New Cairo: 7 minutes from 90th Street, 8 minutes from the American University in Cairo, 13 minutes from Maadi and 19 minutes from Cairo Airport.

Who is the developer of District 5?

The developer is Marakez, which also developed Crescent Walk in the 6th Settlement, Ramla in Ras El Hekma and Shams Soma in Soma Bay.

What unit types and sizes are available at District 5?

District 5 is a mixed-use project on 268 feddans. Its homes are finished apartments from 81 to 343 m² with 1 to 3 bedrooms.

What do District 5 prices start from?

District 5 apartments start from EGP 11,285,000. Prices are indicative and may be changed by the developer.

What are the payment plans at District 5?

Two plans: a 5% down payment with installments up to 8 years, or ready-to-move with a 10% down payment and installments over 7 years.

What facilities and services does District 5 have?

A shopping mall (D5M), a sports club, an entertainment zone (LA7), restaurants and cafés, District5 Work offices and District5 Campus.

What is the difference between District 5 and Crescent Walk?

Both are developed by Marakez in New Cairo. District 5 is a mixed-use project on 268 feddans with ready-to-move apartments, while Crescent Walk is a residential and retail community on 118 feddans in the 6th Settlement. See Crescent Walk details and prices.
