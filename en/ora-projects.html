FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Zed East and Solana East

Are Zed East and Solana East units finished?

Yes, they are fully finished with air conditioning. Zed East apartments are fully finished serviced units with air conditioning, and Gardinn phase apartments at Solana East are fully finished with air conditioning, without kitchen cabinets.

Where are Zed East and Solana East?

Both projects are in New Cairo.

Who is the developer?

ORA Developers, founded in 2016 by Eng. Naguib Sawiris.

What do prices start from?

Zed East starts from EGP 8,900,000 and Solana East from EGP 8,975,000 for a one-bedroom 62 m² apartment in the Gardinn phase. Prices are indicative and may be changed by the developer.

What are the payment plans?

Zed East: down payment from 0% with installments up to 10 years. Solana East: 5% down payment plus 5% on contract, with installments up to 9 years.

When is delivery at Solana East?

The Gardinn phase at Solana East is delivered in 4.5 years.
