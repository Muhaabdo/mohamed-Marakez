FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Shams Soma

Where exactly is Shams Soma?

Shams Soma is in the heart of Soma Bay on the Red Sea in Hurghada, close to The Cascades Golf Resort, Soma Bay Marina and GEMS International School Soma Bay.

Who is the developer of Shams Soma?

The developer is Marakez, and Shams Soma is its Red Sea project. Marakez also developed District 5 and Crescent Walk in New Cairo and Ramla in Ras El Hekma.

What unit types does Shams Soma offer? Chalets or villas?

In the Family & Friends phase, Shams Soma offers 14 unit types across apartments and chalets, townhouses and standalone villas, with built-up areas from 102 to 289 m².

What do Shams Soma prices start from?

Shams Soma apartments and chalets start from EGP 19,500,000, townhouses from EGP 34,000,000 and standalone villas from EGP 46,000,000. Prices are indicative and may be changed by the developer.

What are the payment plans at Shams Soma?

Two plans: 5% down payment plus 5% after 3 months with the balance over 7 years, or 10% down payment with the balance over 8 years. The expression of interest (EOI) is EGP 200,000 for apartments and chalets and EGP 300,000 for villas and townhouses.

What facilities and services does Shams Soma have?

The developer will announce the project's final facilities once they are approved. Around the project in Soma Bay you will find golf courses, Soma Bay Marina, Soma Sports Club and Soma Tennis Academy.

What is the difference between Shams Soma and Ramla?

Both are developed by Marakez. Shams Soma is in Soma Bay on the Red Sea, while Ramla is in Ras El Hekma on the North Coast. See Ramla details and prices.
